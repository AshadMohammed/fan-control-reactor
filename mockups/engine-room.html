Operator’s handbook / quick plate

HOW TO READ THIS PANEL

Read the core first, then check whether the pumps are moving enough air. The live sentence at the top translates the key readings into plain language.

01 / REACTOR DRUMTemperature is the heat in the chip. Below 80 °C is normal; at 95 °C the interlock forces maximum cooling.
02 / PUMPSRPM is fan rotation. The 3D brass rotors show the same speed; visible coolant flecks grow denser as fans speed up.
03 / GOVERNORAuto: Apple runs the fans. Curve: fans rise with temperature between your two set points. Manual: you pick one speed.
04–05 / SET POINTS & LEVERMove the curve start/full values to tune the ramp. The manual lever only works while Manual mode is selected.
06 / WATCHTen minutes of heat, fan speed, and chip power. A brief rise is expected under heavy work; sustained heat needs airflow.
07–09 / ALARMS & SCRAMAcknowledge records that you saw an alarm. SCRAM: lift the cover, then press the red handle. Choose Auto, Curve, or Manual to leave SCRAM.

Good: steady temperature under 80 °C, active cooling when hot, no fault lamps. Bad: CORE HOT, interlock, stalled pump, or lost link.
