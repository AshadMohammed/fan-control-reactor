    How to read this panel

    Read from top to bottom: overall status, alarm windows, heat and airflow, then controls and history. The three-dimensional cutaway brings the temperature and cooling speeds into one view.

    01 · REACTOR VESSELBlue rods are cool. Their glow shifts toward amber and red as the chip heats up. The cylinder is a cutaway pressure vessel.
    02 · CIRCULATION PUMPSBoth impellers spin with measured fan rpm; moving particles show airflow, and become denser as the fans speed up.
    03 · CHIP TELEMETRYPower and load explain why temperature can rise. Under 80 °C is normal operation.
    04 · COOLING CONTROLSAuto: Apple runs the fans. Curve: fans rise with temperature between your set points. Manual: you pick one speed. SCRAM: both fans to full until you change mode.
    05 · HISTORYThe ten-minute trace compares temperature, fan percent and chip watts. A rising temperature line is a cue to check airflow.
    06 · EVENT LOGMode changes, alarms and link changes appear with their time and severity.
    07 · SOUNDOff is silent. Alarms adds a chime. Alarm + Geiger adds power-reactive clicks.
    ALARM WINDOWSWarm begins at 80 °C; Hot at 92 °C; Interlock at 95 °C forces both fans to maximum. Acknowledge stops flashing.
    LAMP TESTLights every annunciator window briefly so you can see that the indicators work.
