HOW TO READ THIS PANEL

Read the top sentence first: it tells you the current heat, fan setting, and whether the system is normal.

01 / CRT VESSEL
The green wireframe is the core; its colour follows heat. The two small turbines show the actual cooling fans and airflow.
02 / INSTRUMENT BANK
Chip temperature is the key reading. Under 80 °C is normal; 80–91 °C is warm; 92 °C and up is hot.
03 / FAN GOVERNOR
Auto: Apple runs the fans. Curve: fans rise with temperature between your two set points. Manual: you pick one speed.
04 / GUARDED SCRAM
Open the cover, then press SCRAM to set both fans to full. Choose Auto, Curve, or Manual to leave SCRAM.
05 / CURVE + HISTORY
The marker shows current temperature on your curve. History compares temperature, fan speed, and chip power across ten minutes.
06 / EVENT TAPE
Recent changes and alarms appear here. Acknowledge flashing alarms after you have read them.
